# Collectionner

## What it is

A one-page Kanban board design, built with the STATIK method. Front-end only: no backend, data lives in memory. It is a first-version design piece to show a team and impress them.

## Audience

A five-person product team (Diego, Luis, Jose Diego, Brenda, Beth) and the people they present to. Spanish-speaking. Beth, Brenda and Jose Diego wear glasses; Luis and Diego do not.

## Job

Read the state of a sprint at a glance and move notes between lanes. A newcomer must understand it without explanation.

## Must exist

- WIP limit above every lane. A full lane refuses a note.
- Two sprints on the same page: Sprint 1 is the MVP, Sprint 2 is the increment. Each has its own lanes.
- Switching sprint shows a unique phrase and a screen-lowering transition.
- The five characters live on the cards, not in a team header, as adult portraits. They react: glad when a note advances, worried and sad when it returns.
- Agent cursors that move by themselves according to task state, at an unhurried pace. Each cursor belongs to one person and only moves that person's notes.
- A unique entrance animation. Fluid, clean motion throughout.
- Responsive.

## Tone

A real working tool for adults: legible, compact, and unique. It plays like a product film. It must not look childish or AI-generated. Rejected so far: uncanny realistic faces (scary), chibi faces (for kids), plain neutral boards (ugly and basic).

## Must not

- Look loaded or generic.
- Move cards so fast the eye cannot follow.
- Invent real metrics or customer claims. Board content is illustrative.

## Mode

Operate.
